A life unfolding.

Kai is curious, observant, and drawn to making things. You begin as their parent, sharing caregiving with your mother.

At 18, the perspective shifts. You make choices as Kai through adulthood.

Family, community, opportunities, and constraints evolve alongside your choices.

Original educational simulation. Outcomes are plausible fictional illustrations, not predictions, diagnoses, or measures of a successful life. No student responses are sent to an instructor or server by this app.
